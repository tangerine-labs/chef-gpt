  • harissa 4 g
  • creme fraiche 1 bæger

  1. Boil the spaghetti; keep a cup of the water.
  2. Fry chorizo and onion until the fat runs.
  3. Stir in harissa and creme fraiche, loosen with the water.
  4. Toss with the spaghetti and serve.
