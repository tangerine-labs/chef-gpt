Set up in three steps

  1. 1 Sign in here with Google or a magic link. That creates your household.
  2. 2 Add the connector in Claude Desktop: Settings → Connectors → Add custom connector, and paste the connector URL shown after you sign in.
  3. 3 Approve. Claude brings you back here to authorize it. Then open a new chat and say "set up my household".
