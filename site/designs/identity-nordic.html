Hurtig chorizo- og harissaspaghetti
20 min · Italian · HelloFresh
  • Spaghetti400 g
  • Chorizo, ternet120 g
  • Harissa4 g
  • Creme fraiche1 bæger
  • Løg1
  1. Boil the spaghetti; keep a cup of the water.
  2. Fry the chorizo until the fat runs, add the onion.
  3. Stir in harissa and creme fraiche, loosen with pasta water.
  4. Toss with the spaghetti and serve.
